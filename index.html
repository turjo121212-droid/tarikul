Research interests

Bacterial diversity and microbial ecology

How bacterial communities are structured in oceanic regimes such as the Arabian Sea oxygen minimum zone and the Southern Ocean subtropical front, and how they respond to micronutrient enrichment.

Bio-electrochemical systems and bio-energy

Extracellular electron transfer in Shewanella loihica PV-4 and Geobacter sulfurreducens biofilms, studied with voltammetry and visible spectroelectrochemistry.

Biofilms and biofouling

Composition of the marine conditioning film, exopolymer chemistry, and the cell-surface properties that control bacterial adhesion.
